## 2. The posture of the two zips (OPEN-D53, posture part; decision A15)

Two steps, both before any DIS item value is read, neither reading a DIS item column:
1. Rule version 2 of the structure probe, written into reports/modys_structure/rule.txt with its SHA-256 before the
   probe computes anything under it. Per training record: q = the median over frames, where the hips, the knees and the
   shoulders are valid (likelihood at least 0.5), of the image distance from the hip midpoint to the knee midpoint over
   the torso length of the same frame (hip midpoint to shoulder midpoint). Per zip: Q = the median of q over its
   records. The zip with the larger Q is read as lying (thighs in the image plane), the other as sitting (thighs toward
   the camera). Settled when Q differs between the zips and at least 90 percent of the records of each zip lie on that
   zip's side of the midpoint of the two Q values; otherwise not settled.
2. The session's viewing: one stick-figure movie per zip of the same training child, the lowest video number held in
   both zips at visit T0, rendered from the keypoints alone; the session writes what it sees (lying or sitting, the
   view) in a new file, reports/modys_structure/viewing.md, before reading the output of step 1.
The posture of each zip is settled when steps 1 and 2 agree. Otherwise it stays open: every output is labelled by zip
name, the outputs that pair a descriptor with a DIS item in its scored posture are withdrawn (draft section 7), and S2
does not run. A settled reading that contradicts the MoveAtlas wording ("side-view lying" in
configs/canonical_skeleton.yaml of the MoveAtlas tree) goes to docs/moveatlas_corrections/ as a candidate.

## 3. The reference posture and the thresholds (OPEN-D57 with OPEN-67; decision A16)

Written before any read, the same for both rest protocols, in the image plane of the frontal view that both zips show:
- Parent axis: the trunk axis, from the shoulder midpoint to the hip midpoint (pointing down the body).
- TON-T of the proximal arm: the absolute angle between the shoulder-to-elbow vector of a side and the parent axis;
  of the proximal leg: the absolute angle between the hip-to-knee vector of a side and the parent axis. The reference
  posture is the anatomical position, 0 deg for both (arm hanging along the trunk, thigh along it). Each angle is
  low-passed at 0.3 Hz as DR-14.2 writes, the record value the median over windows (draft section 3.3).
- The trunk: its angle against the image vertical is reported raw, never as a deviation (draft OPEN-D38 default).
- Dwell threshold (TON-DW): a deviation beyond 10 percent of the joint's frontal-plane range in jarm2022 (the program's
  range-of-motion source, mdfm/synth/limits_ext.py SOURCES), the lowest DIS amplitude band edge: shoulder abduction 0
  to 180 deg gives 18.0 deg for the proximal arm; hip abduction 0 to 45 deg gives 4.5 deg for the proximal leg.
  Occupancy (FLOW-OCC): 10 percent of the segment's image-plane length, as the draft's OPEN-D37 default writes.
- Norm-free values are printed beside every descriptor: the raw angles and the raw image-plane values in torso lengths.
- The lowest-DIS quartile of DR-6.4 is not computed, as primary or secondary: it is defined on the labels S1's outputs
  are set against (decision A16).
- The reference is the session's, written under the delegation; the neurologist reviews it with the project's results
  (deferred by the owner on 2026-11-03), and a change is a new dated version before any read it governs.
