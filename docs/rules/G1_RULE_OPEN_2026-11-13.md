## 2. The truth (replaces the first paragraph of draft section 3 for an open declaration)

Per person, the open body Anny fitted to the person's takes by the rule of plan step M3: mdfm/body/openfit.py
(fit_person, then fit_take on every take) on the landmarks that scripts/build_open_body_store.py fits for gait120 and
stroke_gait_vancriekinge (the 15 canonical joints of the MoveAtlas joint maps in metres with the root kept, and one toe
per foot, read by the readers that build the store), with the phenotype prior's unknown-sex row and the constants of
the store's build. A take is kept when its RMS landmark residual is at most the store's residual bound, 16.7 mm
(reports/open_body_store/fit_measure.json, residual_bound_m 0.01671615061624286, rule "3 x the median RMS of the
measured takes"; kept on the refitted store, reports/open_body_store/bound_recheck.md). A take over the bound is
refused and counted per set and per source; a person without a kept take is unscored and listed. The code SHA-256 of
openfit.py, of the readers and of the store builder's fit path, and the fit constants, enter the input manifest.

From the fitted take on, draft section 3 as written: the 100 Hz clock and the 12 Hz zero-phase fourth-order
Butterworth low-pass applied to the fitted take before forward kinematics (the harness names the function and its code
SHA-256 in the input manifest), forward kinematics of the 22 program joints on the person's fitted rest skeleton
(mdfm/body/openbody.py, the P3 basis), the floor at y = 0, the treadmill rule, the windows (section 4 for their
minimum) and their frames. The fit reads the landmarks, the phenotype prior (reports/open_body_priors/) and the
anatomical joint-limit table (reports/openbody/joint_limits.json); no model of the program enters the truth.

The fits of the development and calibration persons are built before any checkpoint runs on them and cached with
their manifest under cache/g1/g1_open_v1; the fits of the lockbox persons are made only inside the logged read,
through mdfm.gates' lockbox permission (reader flags False elsewhere), and cached under the gates-only store
(g1_open_v1/lockbox), which the window store and the sampler refuse.

1. persons = the person_id strings of P_S, sorted by Python's default string order (code points);
2. seed = int(hashlib.sha256(("20260928:g1_open_halves:" + S).encode("utf-8")).hexdigest()[:16], 16), the form of

## 4. Inputs, skeleton and windows for the open body (adds to draft sections 3, 4 and 10)

- Inputs are rendered from the truth of section 2 by the reference renderers, with the IMU sites of the open body
  (site offsets re-measured on the Anny surface, plan section 3.1) and the mount rotations and device levels of the
  open mount priors (plan step M4; path and SHA-256 in the input manifest) in place of reports/mount_priors/priors.json.
  Every other input setting is draft section 3's.
- Skeleton: the draft's "the model's skeleton is the take's recorded gender" reads, for the open body, the model's
  own phenotype posterior, no recorded value entering. For a declared SMPL+H checkpoint run on these sets (dev only,
  under docs/rules/OPEN_PORT_COMPARISON_RULE_2026-11-13.md), the gender code of the person's recorded sex where the
  source gives it and the neutral code where it does not. Sex is never an encoder input.
- min_window_s reads 1.0 (draft section 10 reads 2.0): the last window of a take is kept when it lasts at least 1 s,
  and a take shorter than 1 s is counted and dropped. Reason: the gait120 and stroke takes are short (gait120 pose3d
  2 h over 3,304 recordings, stroke able-bodied 0.43 h over 586, reports/stage1_sources/summary.md, about 2.2 and 2.6 s
  per recording), so the 2 s minimum set for the long AMASS takes would drop a large share of the takes, the faster
  walkers' first; 1 s is the window store's minimum (mdfm.eval.infer MIN_S, docs/rules/G4_RULE.md section 2), below
  which no window reached either model's training. The counts kept and dropped are reported per set and source. The
  harness's PARAMS for an open declaration differ from the draft's table in this key only, and tests/test_g1.py
  compares that table with this section.
- Inference: the mode ema_s20_nopool of the 2026-11-12 version's section 2; the conformal factors of draft section 6
  are fitted inside the read on the calibration half.
- Trivial baseline and population prior (draft section 7): from the train persons of the open body store (store
  F:/MD_FM/bodies_open_v1 or MDFM_OPEN_BODY_STORE, its index and person-list SHA-256 from its hashes.json in the input
  manifest), never from a G1 set.
- Per-archive rows (draft section 8) become per-source rows (gait120, stroke patients), reported beside every line.

## 5. The question on these sets

Draft section 1 asks whether the model recovers the body of persons it never saw, through its own sensor kernels, from
sparse synthetic sensors; on AMASS that was varied motion (hand-object, sport, dance). On these sets it is gait and
transfers: level walking, slopes, stairs, sit to stand and stand to sit of healthy men (gait120; tasks in
reports/stage1_sources/tasks.csv) and walking of stroke patients. A pass reads "recovers the gait and transfers of
unseen persons, healthy and post-stroke, from sparse synthetic sensors"; it says nothing of varied motion. No G1 number
of these sets compares with the pilot's G1 on AMASS, and none is put beside it as if it did.

## 6. Attainability on these sets (draft section 11, item 11)

Standard of docs/rules/G3_RULE.md section 10, item 6. On 2026-11-13 no predictor, oracle or reference has been
computed on these sets, so every line is NOT SHOWN on them. The evidence for the model class is the pilot's on AMASS
(the 2026-11-12 version, section 4: G1b shown at 0.929, G1c shown at 42.09 mm, G1a not shown). Two cautions: the
stroke patients are in no training set of either model, so the lines may not carry to them, and the per-source rows
will say so; and the truth's joints are Anny's, the open model's own joint definitions, so a declared SMPL+H
checkpoint scores against foreign joint definitions on these sets (the comparison rule corrects for it on dev; this
gate does not). Before the first G1 lockbox read on an open checkpoint, its own dev rows, the trivial baseline's and
the population prior's on the development half are computed and reported beside the read. No line moves on them.
