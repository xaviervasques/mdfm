## 1. The arms (owner decision C8)

Under C8 the MD_FM model moves off SMPL+H and AMASS onto the open body (Anny) and open data, with a full retraining.
This version ties no comparison to a checkpoint, a body model or a training corpus, and says so:
- A comparison reads the arms its declaration names, written before the comparison is computed (a JSON file whose

  baseline trained against another body configuration is not an arm of that comparison.
- The arms trained on 2026-09-28 to 2026-10-18 (the 60M pilot on SMPL+H trained on AMASS, configs/stage1_pilot.yaml,
  and the MFR and JEPA arms trained from it) form one admissible declaration, every seed of it trained and read on
  dev. The port's retrained body and baselines form another, once trained. Each is read under this one rule.
- Every number cited by the draft, the revision or this file is evidence for the lines and their attainability, not a
  declaration.
