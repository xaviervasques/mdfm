## 2. Inference (decision A9; fixes what draft section 4 leaves open)

The mode ema_s20_nopool: the EMA weights; 20 refinement steps of 256 points per stream, gradients stopped; no shape
pooling (each 10 s window is decoded from its own inputs alone); batches of 16 windows; the G1b predictive of draft
section 4 (16 latent samples times 4 decoder draws). The conformal factors of draft section 6 are fitted inside the read
on the calibration set, by the code that scores it, under this mode (the factor file saved on 2026-10-05 was fitted
under ema_s20_pool for the pilot and is not used). The mode was selected on the pilot's dev windows by the criterion of
reports/gates/inference_mode/criterion.json (SHA-256 d71767b815d8da4bad65cfe6e19df7a569e5ad4a8c841f6889819dd57c495fe5,
written 2026-10-05T23:28:17 before the grid ran); its numbers are post-selection (reports/gates/inference_mode/
summary.md, section "Selected mode": G1a 81.6 mm on the full dev run). It is a setting of the read for every declared
checkpoint and is not chosen again on the checkpoint read.
