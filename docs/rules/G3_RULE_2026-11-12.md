## 3. The classical rows and the claim (decision A7: option 1 now, option 3 as the direction)

Added to draft section 7, a reported row of the stride, speed and MAPE lines, deciding nothing:
- Foot-IMU ZUPT (tasks ZB and ZPD; scripts/g3_zupt_baseline.py, scripts/g3_zupt_pd.py): strapdown integration of each
  foot sensor between its still runs (version 2 of task SCALE), matched by the harness itself; two timings, zupt_ff
  (each stride ended at the foot-flat onset) and zupt_seg (that onset minus one lag fitted on the 54 training-fold
  controls, 0.1400 s on the pilot's fold), each also divided by one training-fold gain (zupt_ff_gain, zupt_seg_gain);
  with their matched shares, on the primary row and on the all-trials row. On the lockbox they are computed inside the
  logged read from the records it opens, the lag and the gains fitted on the training fold only. G12 reports them as
  context.

The claim. On foot-instrumented input a label-free integration reaches the stride and speed lines (section 5), so a
pass of those lines shows that the body is not broken, not that it reads what a classical method does not. The body's
G3 claim is therefore stated in three parts:
- the lines as written, each reported beside the ZUPT rows;
- the coverage role: the share of the walkway strides and of the persons that the body and the ZUPT each match on the
  same reference strides, reported on both rows (on the pilot's PD persons, post hoc, the ZUPT matched no stride for 1
  of 74 persons and stayed below 0.9 coverage for 19, reports/weekend_2026-10-01/tasks/ZPD.md);
- the direction of the program (option 3 of docs/proposals/G3_ZUPT_BASELINE_DRAFT.md): what no strapdown integral
  reads, inputs without the foot sensors and whole-body quantities. Each needs its own rule text, trivial baselines and
  attainability before any lockbox read; this version writes none of them and no line of it depends on them.
No margin against the ZUPT is added (option 2 is not taken: no model of the program approaches it).

## 4. The descriptor rows G3 needs (decision A21; descriptor draft section 19)
